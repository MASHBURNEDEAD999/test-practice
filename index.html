SpecForge

Read a course specification, map it to outcomes, check it for defects, and send it for approval.

Reader

Paste a course specification, then extract its structure. Expected lines: Course Code, Course Title, Credits, and CO1, CO2, and so on.

Mapper

Levels: 3 high, 2 medium, 1 low, dash for no link. Click any cell to change its level.

Inspector

Checks the extracted data and the mapping matrix for defects.

Workflow

Move the specification from Author to Reviewer to HOD. Submission is blocked while the Inspector reports errors.
